MAGNETIC FIELD (NOAA WMM)
What it measures: Earth's magnetic field intensity (nT) across a grid around your target. Buried stone, brick, and fired clay distort the local field.
GOOD SIGNAL Gradient > 50 nT across the grid = possible buried structures. High gradient over a small area = likely man-made. Uniform field = nothing buried.
SAR BACKSCATTER (SENTINEL-1)
What it measures: Radar reflectivity (dB) from Sentinel-1. Radar sees through clouds and detects surface roughness changes from buried features.
GOOD SIGNAL VV/VH ratio spikes = surface disruption. Consistent backscatter anomaly across dates = buried structure affecting surface texture.
SOLAR RADIATION (NASA POWER)
What it measures: Surface solar irradiance (W/m²) from NASA POWER. Affects thermal readings — high solar input can mask or amplify subsurface thermal signals.
GOOD SIGNAL Stable irradiance = clean thermal baseline for anomaly detection. Sudden drops = cloud cover (adjust thermal interpretation). High values = strong thermal contrast potential.
MAGNETIC FFT SPECTRUM
What it measures: Spatial frequency of magnetic field along a transect. Detects periodic subsurface features (walls, ditches, roads) via magnetic signature patterns.
GOOD SIGNAL Clean harmonic peaks = periodic buried structures. High magnitude with clear period = man-made origin. Flat = uniform geology.
MAGNETIC INTENSITY MAP
What it measures: 2D magnetic field intensity grid from NOAA World Magnetic Model. Hotspots indicate concentrated magnetic energy from buried ferrous/materials.
GOOD SIGNAL Focused hotspot = likely buried metallic/man-made object. Diffuse gradient = geological variation. Uniform = no subsurface features.
